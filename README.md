## 🌌 The Mission

Close-approach events between satellites are rising sharply as constellations scale into the thousands[cite: 1]. Current operational standards rely heavily on manual conjunction reviews or simple decentralized avoidance rules[cite: 1]. These legacy methods are slow, propellant-wasteful, and increasingly untenable as encounter rates grow[cite: 1]. Conversely, fully learned "black-box" AI solutions cannot be certified or easily explained to regulators and insurers[cite: 1].

**OrbitFlow** provides an intelligence layer, not just a recommendation feed[cite: 1]. We solve the multi-agent traffic management problem by combining the rigorous physical grounding of deterministic search with the scalability of multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL).

## ⚙️ System Architecture

Our solution is built on a hierarchical planning system that pairs an interpretable, physics-grounded planner with a coordination layer[cite: 1].

### 1. The Generator: A* Trajectory Search

* A* search generates a discrete set of safe, low-cost trajectory candidates per satellite[cite: 1].
* Orbital dynamics and collision risk are encoded directly into the search[cite: 1].
* This approach ensures the generated maneuver space is structured and auditable, rather than a black box[cite: 1].

### 2. The Coordinator: Multi-Agent Decision Layer

* A multi-agent coordination layer selects joint maneuvers across satellites[cite: 1].
* This layer is designed to transition from a centralized optimizer to a learned MARL policy[cite: 1].
* By optimizing jointly across all satellites in an encounter, the system minimizes total propellant consumption while enforcing separation constraints[cite: 1].
* Crucially, this avoids the inefficient equilibria that arise when satellites avoid each other independently[cite: 1].

### 3. The Framework: Hybrid ECI/LVLH

* Our hybrid ECI/LVLH architecture keeps the system physically realistic[cite: 1].
* Full orbit propagation and conjunction screening are executed in Earth-Centered Inertial (ECI) coordinates[cite: 1].
* Local relative-motion planning is handled in the Local Vertical Local Horizontal (LVLH) frame to keep the planning problem tractable[cite: 1].

### 4. The Guarantee: Hard Safety Filter

* Safety is treated as a hard guarantee, not a soft suggestion[cite: 1].
* Every maneuver proposed by the MARL layer is checked against a constraint-enforcement safety filter before execution[cite: 1].
* This ensures separation guarantees hold even if the learned policy behaves unexpectedly[cite: 1].

## 🤝 Competitive Advantage

Most space traffic management (STM) companies today sit strictly at the detection and decision-support layer, handing each operator a single-satellite maneuver recommendation[cite: 1]. Even with AI-assisted risk scoring, avoidance decisions are treated independently[cite: 1].

**OrbitFlow** is fundamentally different: our A* planner generates the candidate maneuver space itself, and our multi-agent layer optimizes those maneuvers jointly to eliminate wasted propellant and prevent new, cascading conflicts[cite: 1]. Our structured-planner-plus-coordination-layer design provides a defensible certifiability story that pure end-to-end ML approaches cannot offer[cite: 1].
